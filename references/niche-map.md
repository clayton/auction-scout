## How to Use in Scoring

During Tier 2 or Tier 3 evaluation, check if the expiring domain's name or niche aligns with any property above. A strong match means the domain's backlink profile can be redirected to boost an existing site's authority.

### Redirect Value Bonus (add to Buildability score)

| Match Quality | Bonus | Criteria |
|--------------|-------|----------|
| Strong match | +8 | Domain name contains a niche keyword AND has TF 15+ and 50+ referring domains |
| Moderate match | +5 | Domain is in the same general niche AND has TF 10+ and 20+ referring domains |
| Weak match | +2 | Tangentially related niche, decent backlinks |
| No match | 0 | Domain doesn't align with any existing property |

### Important Notes
- The redirect bonus is IN ADDITION to the normal Buildability score, but the total Buildability dimension is still capped at 35.
- A domain bought purely for redirect value doesn't need a high Product-Market Fit score — the backlinks ARE the value.
- Still check risk factors (blacklisted domains will pass toxic links, active business domains are legal risk).
- The best redirect candidates have: high TF, many referring domains, clean history, and a name that closely matches the target site's niche.
