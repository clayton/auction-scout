## Buildability (0-35 pts)

### Name Quality (0-10 pts)
Evaluate: brandability, length (shorter = better), memorability, keyword relevance, ease of spelling/pronunciation.

| Quality | Points |
|---------|--------|
| Exceptional (short, brandable, keyword-rich) | 8-10 |
| Good (clear meaning, reasonable length) | 5-7 |
| Average (understandable but long or awkward) | 3-4 |
| Poor (confusing, hard to spell, nonsensical) | 0-2 |

### Product-Market Fit (0-15 pts)
**The #1 question: Can you name the exact product, the exact buyer, and point to existing competitors making money?**

If the answer is vague or aspirational, score it low regardless of how cool the domain sounds.

#### The Filter Test
Ask: Does this product help people...
1. **Make more money** (B2B tools, lead gen, sales, freelancer tools)
2. **Look/feel better** (beauty, fitness, diet, fashion, dating)
3. **Solve a painful personal problem** (pets, health, parenting, anxiety, habits)

If it doesn't clearly fit one of these, it's too ambitious for a flip product. Score 0-3 max.

#### Scoring

| Fit | Points | Example |
|-----|--------|---------|
| **Hyper-niche micro-SaaS with obvious buyer** | 13-15 | `customcabinetquotes.com` → quote calculator for cabinet shops, charge $29/mo. Cabinet shops exist, they need quotes, done. |
| **Clear info product in proven spending niche** | 10-12 | `dogobediencemastery.com` → dog training ebook/course. Pet owners spend $$$, dog training is evergreen, tons of existing products prove the market. |
| **Specific affiliate/pSEO in high-intent niche** | 7-9 | `beststandingdesks.com` → review/comparison site with affiliate links. People Google this, click buy, you get commission. Proven model. |
| **Vague but possible — needs a leap of faith** | 4-6 | `imagener.com` → could be an AI image tool? Maybe? But what exactly? Who pays? How is it different from 50 others? Too abstract. |
| **Aspirational / would require a real startup** | 1-3 | Cool-sounding domain but building the product would take a team, funding, or years. |
| **No product fit at all** | 0 | Random word, locked into dead niche, or name doesn't map to anything buildable. |

#### What "Proven Market" Means
- **YES proven**: You can find 3+ competitors already selling a similar product and making money. Real products, real prices, real customers.
- **NOT proven**: "There could be demand for this" or "AI is hot right now" or "people need tools for X" — that's speculation, not proof.

#### Preferred Product Types (concrete, flippable)
1. **Hyper-niche micro-SaaS** — solves one specific problem for one specific type of business. Think: invoice generator for freelance plumbers, not "project management tool."
2. **Info products in spending niches** — ebook, course, or guide in a niche where people already buy (pets, fitness, beauty, dating, parenting, cooking, money/investing, self-help).
3. **Programmatic SEO / affiliate** — review sites, comparison tools, calculators, directories in niches with commercial intent.
4. **Simple iOS utility app** — if the domain maps to a clear single-purpose app (timer, tracker, calculator for a specific thing).

**NOT interested in:** Ecommerce (requires inventory/fulfillment), communities, forums, social networks, marketplaces, general-purpose SaaS, anything requiring network effects.

### Niche Opportunity (0-10 pts)

Score based on **proof of existing demand**, not speculation.

| Signal | Points | How to verify |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Competitors exist and are making money, keywords have volume | 8-10 | Find 3+ paid products in the niche. Check Google Trends, search volume. |
| Some competitors, moderate search volume | 5-7 | 1-2 paid products exist, some organic search traffic in niche. |
| Niche exists but crowded with big players | 2-4 | Market is real but you'd be competing with well-funded companies. |
| No evidence of paying customers in this niche | 0-1 | Can't find anyone selling a similar product. |

## Risk (0-30 pts)

Start at 30 points, then deduct for risk factors found.

### Active Business Using Domain (-15 to -30)
**CRITICAL CHECK.** This is the most important risk factor.

Detection methods:
- `site:domain.com` in Google - if results show active business pages, MAJOR red flag
- Wayback Machine - recent snapshots showing active business
- Social media mentions of the domain as an active service
- DNS records showing active mail servers

| Finding | Deduction |
|---------|-----------|
| Clearly active business with customers | -30 (SKIP domain entirely) |
| Recently active (last 6 months) | -20 |
| Active 1-2 years ago | -15 |
| No evidence of active business | 0 |

### Spam / Blacklist History (-10 to -20)
Check: Spamhaus ZEN, SURBL, URIBL, Google Safe Browsing.

| Finding | Deduction |
|---------|-----------|
| Currently blacklisted | -20 (SKIP domain) |
| Previously blacklisted, now clean | -10 |
| Clean | 0 |

### Trademark Conflict (-10 to -15)
| Finding | Deduction |
|---------|-----------|
| Registered trademark match | -15 (SKIP domain) |
| Possible TM conflict | -10 |
| No TM issues | 0 |

### Penalty Indicators (-5 to -15)
Wayback history showing: doorway pages, PBN content, Japanese/Chinese spam, pharma spam, casino spam.

| Finding | Deduction |
|---------|-----------|
| Heavy spam history | -15 |
| Some spam/PBN history | -10 |
| Minor thin content | -5 |
| Clean history | 0 |

### Thin History (-3)
| Finding | Deduction |
|---------|-----------|
| Parked/unused for most of its life | -3 |
| Normal usage history | 0 |

## Action Thresholds

| Score | Action | Max Bid |
|-------|--------|---------|
| 0-49 | Skip | - |
| 50-64 | Log in report only | - |
| 65-74 | Flag as "interesting", no bid | - |
| 75-84 | Auto-bid | $30 |
| 85-89 | Auto-bid | $50 |
| 90-100 | Auto-bid (outlier) | $100 |

### Budget Guards
- Weekly budget cap: $200 (configurable in state.json)
- Daily spend tracking in state.json
- Never exceed hard cap of $100 per domain regardless of score
- If weekly budget is 80%+ spent, only bid on 90+ scores
